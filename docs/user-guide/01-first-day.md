# 1 · First day: install Baba and set up your company

This takes about 20 minutes. Do it once. You need: a Windows computer, and a pen and paper to write down your password.

---

## Step 1: Install Baba

1. You were given a file called **Baba-Setup-0.1.0.exe**. Double-click it.
2. Windows may show a blue box saying **"Windows protected your PC"**. This happens because Baba is new and has not paid for Microsoft's certificate yet. It is safe if the file came from the person who made Baba for you.
   - Click **More info**.
   - Click **Run anyway**.
3. Click **Next**. Leave the box **Create a desktop shortcut** ticked.
4. Click **Install**, then **Finish**.

✅ You now have a **Baba** icon (a dark blue square with a letter B) on your desktop and in the Start menu.

💡 You do not need an administrator password. Baba installs only for the person who is logged in to Windows.

---

## Step 2: Open Baba

Double-click the **Baba** icon.

✅ You see the page **Welcome to Baba** with two buttons: **New company** and **Open company file…**.

At the top right there are three things you can use at any moment:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **العربية** / **English** | Changes the whole program between Arabic and English. |
| **Text size** | Makes all the writing bigger so it is easier to read. Try **Large** if you like. |
| **Menu** | Settings, keyboard shortcuts and **Close company**. |

---

## Step 3: Make your company

Click **New company**. Baba asks a few questions, one small group at a time. You can go **Back** to any step. **Nothing is created until the last step.**

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Company** | Type the company name in Arabic, in English, or both (one is enough). Choose your **Country**: Baba then chooses the right currency, tax rules and starting accounts. Check the **Fiscal year starts in** month (usually January). |
| **Tax numbers** | Type your tax registration number if your country uses one. It is printed on your invoices. Baba checks that it looks right. Some countries need none: just click **Next**. |
| **Address** | Your address, shown on printed invoices. You can change it later. |
| **Accounts** | Choose the starting list of accounts. Pick the one suggested for your country. (An "account" is just a named place where money is counted, such as "Cash" or "Rent".) |
| **Modules** | Switch on the extra parts you need: **Bank and cash**, **Customers and suppliers**, **Sales**, **Purchases**, **Inventory** (stock), **Fixed assets**, **Payroll and employees**, **Expense claims**, **Budgets**, **Cost centers and projects**. Switch on only what you will use. You can change this later in **Settings**; nothing is lost. |
| **Password and file** | Choose the password and where to save the file. See below. |

### ⚠️ The password: the most important minute of your day

- The password locks your company file. You type it every time you open the company.
- **It cannot be recovered.** If you forget it, nobody can open the file, not even the people who made Baba.
- **Write it down on paper** and keep the paper somewhere safe, away from the computer.
- Use something you will remember but others will not guess. At least 8 characters.

### Where to save the file

- In the box **Save the company file as**, type the full location, for example `C:\Books\My Company.baba`, or click **Choose location…** and pick a folder.
- Choose a folder that you **back up regularly** (a folder that is also copied to a USB stick or a cloud drive such as OneDrive).
- Do not rename the ending `.baba`.

Click **Create company**. Wait a few seconds.

✅ You arrive at the **Summary** page, which shows your company name at the top.

---

## Step 4: A first look around

The **menu on the left** is how you move around. It only shows the parts you switched on and the parts your role allows.

| Menu item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Your starting page: money in the bank, what customers owe you, what you owe, profit this month, and buttons to record something. |
| **Chart of accounts** | The list of accounts. |
| **Payments** / **Receipts** / **Journal vouchers** | The three ways to record money going out, money coming in, and other adjustments. |
| **Bank and cash**, **Transfers** | Your bank and cash accounts; moving money between them. |
| **Customers** / **Suppliers** | The people who owe you / whom you owe. |
| **Sales** / **Purchases** | Quotes, orders, invoices, bills. |
| **Products and prices** | What you sell or buy, and price lists. |
| **Stock**, **Warehouses** | What you have on the shelves. |
| **Employees**, **Payroll**, **Expense claims** | People and their pay. |
| **Budgets**, **Fixed assets**, **Cost centers** | Planning, equipment, projects. |
| **Recurring** | Invoices and entries Baba makes again by itself every week, month or year. |
| **Reports** | Every report, ready to print or send to Excel. |
| **Opening balances**, **Year-end**, **Exchange rates** | Starting figures, closing a year, foreign currencies. |
| **Approvals** | Things waiting for someone to approve (only if your company uses approval). |
| **Users and roles**, **Audit log** | Who can use Baba and what they may do; who changed what (owner only). |
| **Settings** | Language, text size, printing, modules, backups, locked months, security. |

On every page there is a **Help** button next to the title. Click it for a short explanation of that page.

---

## Step 5: Put in your starting figures (once)

On the day you begin using Baba, your business already has money, debts and stock. Tell Baba what they were:

1. Click **Opening balances** in the menu.
2. Write what the business had on the day before you start: **cash**, **bank**, **what customers owe you**, **what you owe suppliers**, the owner's **capital**.
3. The two sides (debit and credit) must be equal. Baba tells you when they are not.
4. Click **Save and post**.

💡 Ask your accountant to do this step with you. It is the only step that needs some accounting knowledge.

If you keep stock, record the starting stock on the **Stock** page (see [the storekeeper guide](06-storekeeper.md)). If you have a list of customers or products in Excel, you can bring it in with the **Import…** button on the page (see below).

### Bringing in a list from Excel

On the pages **Chart of accounts**, **Customers**, **Suppliers**, **Products and prices**, **Employees**, **Fixed assets**, **Budgets**, **Exchange rates** and **Opening balances**, there is an **Import…** button.

1. Click **Import…**.
2. Click the link to **download the template**, an Excel file with the right column names.
3. Fill it in (or copy your list into it) and save.
4. Choose the file. Baba shows what it understood and checks every line.
5. If **one line is wrong, nothing is imported**. Baba tells you which line and why. Fix the file and try again. This protects you from half-finished lists.

---

## Step 6: Make a backup now

A backup is a complete copy of your company in one file, with the same password.

- On the **Summary** page click **Back up now**, then choose where to save.
- Or go to **Settings** and find the card **Backups**.

Do this regularly (the routines page says when), and keep one copy somewhere else (USB stick, cloud drive, a second computer). See [Routines](08-routines.md).

### Going back to a backup

1. Click **Menu**, then **Close company**.
2. On the welcome page click **Restore a backup…**.
3. Choose the backup file, then choose a new name for the restored company.
4. Open it with its password.

The restored company is a **new file**. Your current file is not touched.

---

## Step 7: Opening Baba the next day

1. Double-click the **Baba** icon.
2. Under **Recent companies**, click your company.
3. Type the **file password**.

💡 You can also double-click the company file itself (the file with the ending `.baba`). Baba opens and asks for the password.

If your company has user accounts (see [the owner guide](03-owner.md)), Baba then asks you to **sign in** with your own name and password.

When you finish: click **Menu**, then **Close company**, and close the window. Your work is saved all the time, you never need to press "save the file".

---

## If the owner wants other people to use Baba

Everyone who uses the same computer and the same file can use Baba. To give each person their own sign-in and their own rights (for example, the storekeeper cannot see the bank), the owner turns on **user accounts**. That is explained in [the owner guide](03-owner.md) and takes about five minutes.
