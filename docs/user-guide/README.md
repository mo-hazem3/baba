# Baba: the user guide

> 🇸🇦 **الدليل بالعربية:** [ar/README.md](ar/README.md)

**Baba** keeps the accounts of your business on your own computer: what you sell, what you buy, what customers owe you, what you owe, what is in the bank and what is in stock. It works in Arabic and English, and it does not need the internet.

This guide is for the people who use Baba every day. You do not need to know anything about computers or accounting software. Every page says what to click and what you will see.

## Which guide is mine?

| If you are... | Read | What you can do in Baba |
|---|---|---|
| The **owner** or the person who manages the whole company | [03 · Owner and administrator](03-owner.md) | Everything. You also decide who else can use Baba and what each person may do. |
| The **accountant** or the person who keeps the books | [04 · Accountant](04-accountant.md) | Everything about the money: vouchers, bank, reports, tax, payroll, year-end. Not the list of users. |
| In **sales**: you write quotes and invoices | [05 · Sales](05-sales.md) | Customers, quotes, orders, invoices, products and prices. You do not see the company's money reports. |
| The **storekeeper**: you look after the stock | [06 · Storekeeper](06-storekeeper.md) | Stock, warehouses, counting and moving goods. You can look at products and documents. |
| Someone who only needs to **look** (a partner, a bank, an auditor) | [07 · Viewer](07-viewer.md) | Open and read everything about the business. Change nothing. |

Your company can also make its **own roles** (for example "Clerk" or "Cashier") with exactly the rights it wants. The owner guide explains how. If your job has a special name in your company, ask the owner which of the five guides is closest.

## How the guides are written

- **Bold words** are the names you see on the screen: buttons, menu items, page titles.
- ✅ marks "this is what you should see".
- ⚠️ marks something worth being careful about.
- 💡 marks a small tip.
- Everything in these guides is shown in English. In Arabic, Baba shows the same screens right to left; the names are translated, the steps are the same. Press the **العربية** button at the top to switch language at any time. Press **English** to come back.

## What Baba does not do yet

Be honest with yourself before relying on it for these:

- It does **not send e-invoices to the tax authority** for you. It prints a proper tax invoice (with the QR code where your country needs it), but the sending itself is still done the way you do it today.
- It does **not produce the bank files** that some countries use to pay salaries (such as WPS or Mudad files). It works out and records the salaries and prints payslips.
- It does **not** keep **photos or scans of receipts** attached to invoices or expense claims yet.
- It does **not** yet cost stock by "first in, first out". It uses the **average cost**.
- The **social insurance rates** and **end-of-service rules** that come ready-made for each country are starting values. Your accountant must check them against the current law before the first payroll.
- You **cannot change the file password** after the company is made. Choose it carefully.
- It runs on **one computer at a time**. Two people cannot work in the same company file at the same moment. (A shared online version is planned for later.)

## A promise about your data

- Everything is in **one file** on your computer, with the ending `.baba`. It is locked with a password.
- Nobody can open it without the password. **Not even us.** If the password is forgotten, the data cannot be recovered. See [First day](01-first-day.md) for how to keep it safe.
- Baba saves a **copy automatically** before it changes your file to a newer version and before it closes a year. You can also make a copy yourself at any time with one click.
