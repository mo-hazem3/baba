# 10 · Words explained

Accountants use many words. Here are the ones you meet in Baba, in plain language. They are in the order a business meets them.

---

## The basics

| Word | In plain words |
|---|---|
| **Company file** | The one file (ending `.baba`) where all your business data lives. Locked with the **file password**. |
| **Backup** | A complete copy of the company file. If the computer breaks, the backup saves you. |
| **Account** | A named place where money is counted, for example "Cash", "Rent", "Sales". |
| **Chart of accounts** | The list of all your accounts. |
| **Group account** | An account that only holds other accounts (like a folder). You record on the accounts inside it. |
| **Voucher** | One record of something that happened with money: a **receipt** (money in), a **payment** (money out), a **journal** (an adjustment), or a **transfer** (between your own accounts). |
| **Draft** | A voucher or invoice you saved but did not make real yet. Not in your reports. |
| **Posting** (or **Save and post**) | Making it real in the books. It gets a number. |
| **Debit** and **credit** | The two sides of an entry. Every entry has equal debits and credits; that is how the books stay in balance. Baba warns you when they are not equal. |
| **Fiscal year** | Your accounting year. Usually January to December, but it can start in any month. |
| **Locked month** | A finished month. Nobody can add, change or delete anything dated in it. |
| **Opening balances** | What the business had on the day you started with Baba. |

## People who owe and are owed

| Word | In plain words |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | Someone who buys from you and may owe you money. |
| **Supplier** | Someone you buy from and may owe money to. |
| **Receivable** ("owed to you") | Money customers still have to pay you. |
| **Payable** ("you owe") | Money you still have to pay suppliers. |
| **Aging** | A list showing how old each debt is: 0–30 days, 31–60, 61–90, over 90. Old debts are the risky ones. |
| **Statement** | The list of everything bought and paid by one customer or supplier, with the balance. |
| **Credit limit** | The most a customer is allowed to owe you. Baba warns you if an invoice goes over it. |
| **Payment terms** | The number of days a customer has to pay. |

## Selling and buying

| Word | In plain words |
|---|---|
| **Quote** | A price you offer. Changes nothing in the books. |
| **Order** | A customer (or you) agreed. Changes nothing in the books. |
| **Delivery note** | A paper that says goods were handed over. |
| **Invoice** | A request for payment. It is what makes the customer owe you. |
| **Bill** | A supplier's invoice to you. |
| **Credit note** | The opposite of an invoice: you reduce what the customer owes (a return, a mistake). |
| **Debit note** | The same on the supplier side. |
| **Issue** | Make an invoice final and give it its number. |
| **Tax code** | A rule such as "standard rate 15%" that a line can carry. |
| **VAT / tax return** | The tax you charged minus the tax you paid, to report to the tax office. |
| **Exchange rate** | How many units of your currency one unit of a foreign currency is worth. |
| **Exchange gain or loss** | The difference when the rate changed between the invoice and the payment. Baba books it for you. |
| **Price list** | A set of prices that a chosen customer receives. |

## Bank

| Word | In plain words |
|---|---|
| **Reconciliation** | Checking, line by line, that your records agree with the bank's statement. |
| **Statement balance** | The closing balance the bank printed. |
| **Difference** | What is still unexplained. It must be zero before you finish. |

## Stock

| Word | In plain words |
|---|---|
| **Stock item** | A product you count. |
| **Warehouse** | A place where you keep stock. |
| **On hand** | How many you have now. |
| **Average cost** | What one unit cost on average, including old and new purchases. |
| **Stock count** | Counting the shelves and telling Baba the real numbers. |
| **Reorder level** | The quantity at which Baba warns you to buy more. |
| **Cost of goods sold** | What the goods you sold cost you. Baba books it when the invoice is issued. |

## People and pay

| Word | In plain words |
|---|---|
| **Payslip** | The statement of one employee's pay for one month. |
| **Payroll run** | The month's payslips for everybody. |
| **Social insurance** | The contributions by employee and employer that the law of your country requires. |
| **End-of-service** | The pay due to an employee when they leave, which builds up over the years. Baba keeps a **provision**: money set aside in the books. |
| **Expense claim** | An employee asks to be paid back for something they paid for the business. |
| **Fixed asset** | Equipment, vehicles, furniture that lasts for years. |
| **Depreciation** | Spreading the cost of an asset over its life. Baba posts it each month. |
| **Budget** | The plan: how much you expect to earn and spend each month. |
| **Cost center** | A tag (a branch, a project) that lets you see profit for each. |

## The reports

| Word | In plain words |
|---|---|
| **Trial balance** | Every account with its total. The two sides must agree. |
| **Profit and loss** | What you earned minus what you spent in a period. |
| **Balance sheet** | What you own, what you owe, and what is left for the owners, on one date. |
| **General ledger** | Every entry on every account. |
| **Retained earnings** | Profits of earlier years kept in the business. |
| **Year-end closing** | Moving the year's profit or loss into retained earnings and locking the year. |

## People using Baba

| Word | In plain words |
|---|---|
| **User** | A person who signs in. |
| **Role** | A set of rights given to a user: Administrator, Accountant, Sales, Storekeeper, Viewer, or one made by the company. |
| **Approve** | The right to make things final (post, approve, lock). |
| **Approval** | When switched on, people without the Approve right send their work for someone else to post. |
| **Audit log** | The record of every change: who, when, and old and new values. Nobody can edit it. |
