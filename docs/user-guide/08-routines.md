# 8 · Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly routines

A small habit done on time prevents a big problem later. Print this page and stick it near the computer.

Each line says **who** does it. "Owner" can do every line.

---

## Every day

| Who | What | Where in Baba |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Read the four cards and the yellow warnings. | **Summary** |
| Accountant | Record today's **receipts** and **payments**. | **Receipts**, **Payments** |
| Sales | Issue today's **invoices**. Save unfinished ones as drafts. | **Sales** |
| Storekeeper | Check the **reorder warning**. Enter goods that arrived. | **Summary**, **Stock** |
| Accountant or owner | **Approve** what is waiting (if your company uses approval). | **Approvals** |
| Accountant or owner | At the end of the day: **Back up now**. Copy the backup to a USB stick or cloud folder. | **Summary** → **Back up now** |
| Everybody | **Close company** and close Baba when you finish. | **Menu** → **Close company** |

💡 Do not leave Baba open on a shared computer overnight: only one person can have the company open at a time.

---

## Every week

| Who | What |
|---|---|
| Accountant | Open **Customers aging**. Phone the customers who are late. |
| Accountant | Open **Suppliers aging**. Plan what to pay this week. |
| Accountant | Make sure there are **no old drafts** left (the Summary lists them). Post or delete them. |
| Sales | Look at open **quotes** older than two weeks: follow up or delete. |
| Owner | Look at the **audit log** quickly (**Audit log**): anything you do not recognise? |
| Owner | Check that the **backup copies** really exist outside the computer. |

---

## Every month (the "month-end")

Do these in this order. Lock the month at the end, so figures cannot move.

| # | Who | What | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Everybody | Post all drafts dated in the month. | **Summary** warning, voucher lists |
| 2 | Accountant | Enter **bank charges, interest** and other items from the bank statement. | **Payments** / **Receipts** |
| 3 | Accountant | **Reconcile each bank account** to the statement. Difference must be zero. | **Bank and cash** → **Reconcile** |
| 4 | Storekeeper | **Count the stock** and record the difference. | **Stock** → **Count or adjust stock** |
| 5 | Accountant | Make sure **depreciation** is posted (it posts by itself when you open the company). | **Fixed assets** |
| 6 | Accountant | Make the **payroll month**: check the payslips, **Post to the books**, then **Pay salaries**. | **Payroll** |
| 7 | Accountant | Approve and pay **expense claims**. | **Expense claims** |
| 8 | Accountant | Check the reports: **Trial balance**, **Profit and loss**, **Balance sheet**. Look for strange figures. Check that **Stock valuation** shows a ✓. | **Reports** |
| 9 | Accountant | If your country has tax: **Tax return** for the period. It must show a ✓ (agrees with the books). File it the way you do today. | **Reports** → **Tax return** |
| 10 | Owner | Read **Profit and loss** and **Budget versus actual**. | **Reports** |
| 11 | Accountant or owner | **Lock the month.** | **Settings** → **Locked months** → **Lock** |
| 12 | Owner | Make a **backup** and keep it as "End of month" (name the file with the month). | **Back up now** |

⚠️ After a month is locked, nobody can add or change anything dated in it. If you must, **Unlock** (needs the Approve right), correct, **Lock** again. Every step is in the audit log.

---

## Every quarter

- Review **user accounts** (**Users and roles**): has anybody left? Switch them off with **Can sign in** off.
- Review **exchange rates** if you use foreign currencies.
- Do a **restore test**: restore the latest backup as a new company file (**Restore a backup…** on the welcome page), open it with the password, check it looks right, then delete it. A backup you never tested may not work when you need it.

---

## Every year

| # | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Everybody | Make sure every month of the year is **posted and locked**. |
| 2 | Accountant | Count the stock on the last day of the year. |
| 3 | Accountant | Review **fixed assets**: sell or scrap what is gone. |
| 4 | Accountant | Print the year's reports: **Profit and loss**, **Balance sheet**, **Trial balance**, **Customers aging**, **Suppliers aging**. |
| 5 | Owner | **Year-end** → **Close year**. Baba puts the profit or loss into retained earnings, locks the twelve months and saves a copy of the file first. |
| 6 | Owner | Keep that backup of the closing for as many years as the law of your country requires (often 5 to 10). |
| 7 | Accountant | Update the **social insurance rates** in the payroll settings if the law changed. |
| 8 | Owner | Plan the new **budget**: **Budgets** → choose the new year. **Copy** with a percentage helps. |

If you find a mistake after closing: **Year-end** → **Reopen** (only the latest closed year can be reopened), correct it, close again.

---

## When a new person joins or leaves

**Joins:** owner → **Users and roles** → **+ New user** → give the right **role** and a temporary password.
**Leaves:** owner → **Users and roles** → **Edit** → switch off **Can sign in**. (Do not delete: the audit log keeps their name either way.) ⚠️ Baba cannot change the **file password** yet. So tell only people you trust the file password, and give everyone else only their own user name and password. If someone who knew the file password leaves, keep the company file away from them (their copy of the file is theirs only if they have a copy).
