# 7 · Viewer

**Written for:** someone who needs to **read** the company's figures but must change nothing: a business partner, a bank officer, an auditor, a manager.

**Your rights (ready-made Viewer role):** you can **see** everything about the business: accounts, vouchers, customers, invoices, stock, payroll, reports. You cannot add, change, delete, post or approve anything. You cannot see the list of users or the audit log, and you can only look at Settings.

---

## How you get in

1. The owner gives you three things: the **company file** (or tells you where it is), the **file password**, and your own **user name and password**.
2. Double-click **Baba**, click the company under **Recent companies** (or double-click the `.baba` file), type the **file password**.
3. On the sign-in page type your **user name** and your **password**.
4. The first time, Baba asks you to **choose your own password** (at least 8 characters). Choose one only you know.

✅ You arrive at the **Summary**. The menu on the left shows everything you may read.

💡 If two people open the same file at the same time, the second one sees "This company is already open in another window." Only one person can have the file open at a time. Ask the other person to close it (**Menu** → **Close company**), or ask for a copy of the file.

---

## Where to look for what

| You want to know... | Go to |
|---|---|
| How much money does the business have right now? | **Summary**, the card **Cash and bank** |
| What do customers owe, and how late are they? | **Summary** (the card **Owed to you**), then **Reports** → **Customers aging** |
| What does the business owe? | **Summary** (**You owe**), **Reports** → **Suppliers aging** |
| Is the business making money? | **Reports** → **Profit and loss** (choose the dates; tick **Compare with last year**) |
| What is the business worth on a date? | **Reports** → **Balance sheet** |
| Does the bookkeeping add up? | **Reports** → **Trial balance** |
| What happened on one account? | **Reports** → **Account statement**, then choose the account |
| One customer's or supplier's history | **Customers** or **Suppliers**, open one, **Statement** |
| What was invoiced? | **Sales** (and **Purchases**), choose the tab for the kind of document |
| What is in stock and what is it worth? | **Stock**, or **Reports** → **Stock valuation** |
| What equipment does the business own? | **Fixed assets** |
| Staff cost | **Reports** → **Payroll summary** |
| Tax | **Reports** → **Tax return** |

### Going deeper

- On any report, **click an amount**: Baba shows the entries behind it, and one more click opens the voucher or invoice itself.
- Pick the **dates** at the top of a report to look at any period.

---

## Taking figures away

- **Export** (top right of every report and list): choose the **language** (Arabic, English or both), then **PDF**, **Excel** or **CSV**.
- **PDF** opens in a window that you can print or save.
- **Excel** gives real numbers and real dates, which you can add up in your own sheets.

---

## What you will see but cannot do

Baba hides the **+ New** buttons from you. When you open an existing voucher or invoice, buttons such as **Save** may still be shown; if you press one, Baba answers "Your role does not allow this." Nothing is changed. The menu does not show the pages that are not for you.

If you notice a mistake, tell the accountant: do not try to correct it yourself.

---

## When you are done

Click **Menu** → **Sign out** (so the next person must sign in), or **Menu** → **Close company** and close the window.
